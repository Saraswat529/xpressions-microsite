Counts appear here once the site is connected to its Supabase database. It takes about ten minutes:

  1. Create a free project at supabase.com.
  2. In the project, open SQL Editor, paste the contents of supabase/schema.sql and click Run.
  3. Open Project Settings → API and copy the Project URL and the anon public key.
  4. Paste both into js/config.js (supabaseUrl and supabaseAnonKey) and publish the site.

Until then, visitors' picks are saved on their own devices and are sent to the database automatically on their next visit after it is connected.
