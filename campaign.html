Marketing experiment: A/B headline test

Running

Question: does a personal, invitation-style headline get more visitors to register their interest than the original three-word headline?

How it works: each new visitor is randomly shown version A or B of the home page headline, 50/50, and keeps that version on return visits. We compare the share of visitors who click "Register Your Intent Now" (primary metric) and who go on to register interest in at least one event (secondary metric). Preview a version with ?ab=a or ?ab=b. Preview visits are not counted.

Waiting for data.

Ethics: both headlines are truthful and lead to the same content. No personal data is collected; visitors are identified only by a random ID in their browser. Browsers sending Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control are excluded. The test is disclosed in the site's FAQ ("Does this site track me?").
