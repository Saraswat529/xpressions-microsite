How each metric is defined

User: one browser, identified by a random anonymous ID.

Session: one visit; a new one starts when the browser tab session ends or a tracked link is opened.

Engaged session: lasted 10+ seconds in view, had 2+ page views, registered interest or downloaded the schedule (the GA4 definition).

Engagement rate: engaged sessions ÷ sessions.

Engagement time: seconds the page was actually visible on screen; capped at 30 minutes per session.

Page view: each switch to Home, Highlights, Programme, Competitions, Register or FAQ (the site's pages are routes on one URL).

Registration start: the session reached the event picker (Register or Competitions page) or opened an event's details.

Completed registration: a tap on "Count Me In" (or similar) that was saved. This is interest, not a ticket.

Conversion rate: sessions with at least one completed registration ÷ sessions.

Search: impressions, clicks and CTR

Data appears after Google crawls the site, usually within a few days.
